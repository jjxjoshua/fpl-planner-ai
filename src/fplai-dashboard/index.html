Reading the mockup

What is real, and what is a placeholder

The screen above is deliberately built so the hatching maps onto the project's actual state. Solid cells are query results — names, prices, ownership, the ownership trajectories, effective ownership and captaincy share, GW1 minutes, points, xG, xA and defensive contributions, the GW3 match market, and the scoring constants used to shape the distribution all come out of data/store/. Hatched cells are invented, and the hatch colour says why: violet where a model exists but nothing serves it into a planner, orange where the model itself does not exist yet.

What changed since the 24 August draw

  • The EO column went teal. It was the one orange cell that could never be recovered later; the first sample landed 25 Aug and the numbers on this screen are computed from it, not modelled.
  • Phase 2 closed. Six models, 16 scored outcomes: 3 PASS, 11 PASS-with-accepted-departure, 2 BASE-RATE-ONLY, 0 FAIL. Four of the violet columns here did not exist five days ago.
  • GW3 fixtures arrived sideways — through the odds feed rather than the fixture ingest, which is still stuck on matchweek 1.
  • The anytime-goal column went the other way. It was solid on 24 Aug; for a GW3 decision there is no capture at all.

The one placeholder that is still time-critical

GW1 is banked. GW2 is not — the sample is due Tuesday 1 Sep, evening local, once the gameweek settles, and it takes about 83 minutes.

It stays deliberately manual because heartbeat data shows the machine is reliably off across the morning and, since 26 Aug, across the small hours too. A scheduled run inside that window neither fires nor catches up. The same off-window now straddles the 01:30 local deadline, so the most valuable ownership snapshots of the week are being missed as well.

Why xPts is still orange with every input built

All six models pass their gate independently. A player's gameweek score is not independent of them — minutes gate everything, a goal and a clean sheet are negatively correlated through the same match, bonus is a rank within a fixture rather than a property of a player.

Convolving six marginals as if they were independent produces a distribution that is confidently wrong in the tail, which is exactly the region a rank-aware objective is bought for. The two curves in the detail panel are that naive convolution, drawn with the real scoring constants — a shape to argue about, not a number to trust.

What the EO sample immediately showed

  • Haaland at 121.7 EO from 70.3% ownership — a 51-point premium bought entirely by the armband, at a 50.6% captaincy share. Not captaining him is a live, expensive, and now measurable decision.
  • Ownership and EO diverge in both directions. Calvert-Lewin owned by 32.2% carried only 27.1 EO — benched or auto-subbed by 18% of the managers who owned him. Isak went the other way, 17.0% owned to 20.2 EO.
  • Captaincy is a two-horse race and the second horse is cheap. B.Fernandes took 17.6% of armbands; nobody else cleared 7%.

Where the season actually is

GW1: 43 against a 50 average, overall rank 6,382,778, 75th percentile. GW2 in progress and going well — 26 points from the captain alone with ten XI players still to play, rank provisionally 4,461,715 and the 40th percentile.

The one transfer made, Gibbs-White to Rogers, was decided on fixture rather than fitness — specifically so the call resolved before the user slept, instead of depending on a Friday presser landing after a 01:30 deadline. That shape is the requirement, not the anecdote.

The blocking condition on the next phase

The cards model passes its gate and is still not safe to consume. NONE and YELLOW come back overconfident — slopes 0.592 and 0.630, confidence intervals nowhere near 1.0 — and an optimiser that inherits an over-claiming probability makes over-confident transfers with it.

The remedy is already proven in this codebase: the minutes model went from slope 1.114 and ECE 0.0507 to 0.972 and 0.0131 with a nested out-of-sample isotonic layer. Phase 3 does not start on cards until that layer exists.
