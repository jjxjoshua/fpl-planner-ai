## 2. Open questions — the Architect asked, you haven't answered

**Q1 · Is "top 10% overall, moderate aggression" right?**
You said "better rank globally". I turned that into a specific target and a specific risk
curvature in blueprint §10, and it now drives the whole objective function. Never confirmed.
If you actually want top-100k and are willing to eat the variance, the optimiser tunes

**Q2 · Do we pay for odds history?**
The free tier has no historical odds, so **odds can never enter the backtest** — they're a
live-only overlay whose value we can never prove, only assume. A paid tier would let us
validate the claim. Cost vs. rigour, your call.

**Q3 · Do we upgrade API-Football?**
Free tier is locked to seasons 2022–24, and trickles at 100 req/day. Historical lineups are
the ground truth the minutes model trains on — the highest-ROI model in the system. We can
work with 2022–24, but it caps how good minutes ever gets.

**Q4 · Understat — revisit or leave dropped?**
You said you'd dig into the `robots.txt` question yourself. Currently dropped in favour of
`olbauday`. No urgency; flagging so it doesn't vanish.

**Q5 · Is the non-playing enabler structure deliberate?**
Your GW1 squad carries ~£13.0m in players who won't start (Dubravka, Kusi-Asare, possibly
Walle Egeli). One enabler is standard, three is a hole in the XI. If that's a deliberate
strategy I should optimise around it rather than against it.

**Q6 · How much of your week do you want this to take?**

is a different build from one that needs two hours.
